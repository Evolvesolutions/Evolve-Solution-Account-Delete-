Evolve Solution – Account & Data Deletion Request

This page explains how users of apps published by Evolve Solution on the Google Play Store can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Email us at evolvesolutionspvtltd@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Account & Data Deletion Request
  3. Include the following details:
    • Registered email address
    • App name
    • Reason for deletion (optional)

Data That Will Be Deleted

After verification, we will permanently delete:

  • User account information
  • Personal details (name, email, phone number if provided)
  • App usage data linked to the account
  • Any content stored within the app and associated with the account

Data That May Be Retained

Certain data may be retained for a limited period or permanently if required for:

  • Legal or regulatory compliance
  • Fraud prevention and security purposes
  • Accounting or audit obligations

Retention Period

  • Account and personal data: deleted within 7–30 days
  • Backup data (if any): removed during the next system cleanup cycle
